MBTI Carousel Scheduler

A personal tool for scheduling MBTI image carousels to TikTok drafts.

This site hosts the images for a personal command-line tool that sends the operator's own MBTI-themed image carousels to their own TikTok drafts, where they are reviewed and published manually. It is not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.
